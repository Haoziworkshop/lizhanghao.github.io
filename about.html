About

I am a lecturer at the School of Journalism and Communication, Guangzhou University, working at the intersection of communication research, computational social science, and artificial intelligence.

Bio

My research examines how generative AI and algorithmic systems influence persuasion, information exposure, judgment, and social interaction. I am especially interested in combining communication theory with experimental and computational approaches that make platform behavior empirically observable.

I received my bachelor's degree from Jinan University, master's degree from Renmin University of China, and PhD in Communication from Beijing Normal University.
