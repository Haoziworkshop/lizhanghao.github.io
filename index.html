Current questions

A selection of questions currently organizing my research agenda.

Personalization without runaway amplification?

Auditing how recommender systems react to user preferences, and when algorithmic memory amplifies, bounds, or suppresses them.

When AI becomes a “certainty producer”

Examining how answer-based health communication changes uncertainty, checking behavior, and cyberchondria.

AI, counter-speech, and human participation

Testing whether AI-generated counter-speech crowds out or stimulates subsequent human responses in online environments.
